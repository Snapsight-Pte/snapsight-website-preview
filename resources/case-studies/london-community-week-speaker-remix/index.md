## The story

### The challenge

49 speakers covered everything from community-led growth to member retention. Turning those sessions into social posts by hand means watching every talk back, pulling lines, writing captions and making cards, then doing it again for two more official Pages, on a lean team already moving on to the next event.

### What they ran

As an event partner of London Community Week 2026, Snapsight ran Speaker Remix across the full roster: a card for every speaker with their name, their key point and the event’s identity, including its headline sponsor. The 49 stories went out on three official Pages, London Community Week, Community Week and Led by Community C.I.C., and the 48th speaker got the same care as the opening keynote.

### What happened

147 official LinkedIn posts drew 450+ visible engagements, an estimated 20,000 views and 48 reposts into new networks. Speakers’ ideas kept showing up in feeds for weeks after the event.
