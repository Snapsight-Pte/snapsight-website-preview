We’re mostly volunteers. How many people does it take?

One or two. Two ran Festival of Curiosity’s 130+ activities: one started sessions, the other edited in real time. One volunteer ran BEIA’s conference with Snapsight.

Can we run it with no AV team?

Yes. At WiT Japan & North Asia 2024, one person handled the AV, the setup and the integration. Each room needs its sound sent to a laptop running Snapsight, with a reliable internet connection. How it works

How do attendees find it?

By QR code. CityDNA printed it on every badge and reached 65% of attendees. BEIA showed it on the entrance screens and in the breaks, put it on table sheets and in the conference app, and the MC explained it each morning.

Our speakers switch languages mid-sentence. Does that work?

Yes. Snapsight follows a speaker who switches language mid-sentence. Festival of Curiosity ran German and English, Austrian dialect included.

Can attendees read in another language?

Yes, each on their own phone. GSTC2025, the Global Sustainable Tourism Conference in Fiji, offered multilingual summaries to delegates from more than 40 countries.

How do speakers share their session?

Each speaker gets a private Speaker Remix link, chooses their takeaways and reviews the card and the post. Nothing is shared until they choose to share it.

What’s free for community events?

Live text translation for every event: every room at once, up to 10 languages per session, and 100 sessions a year. Takeaways, reports and Speaker Remix are paid. See pricing

Do visitors need an app?

No. They scan the QR code and read in their browser.
