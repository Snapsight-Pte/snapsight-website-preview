## Frequently asked questions

### Can I try Snapsight without booking a demo?

Yes. The experience page opens a real IMEX Frankfurt 2025 session on your phone with no form and no app: live text, takeaways, Idea Cloud and summary, in the language you pick.

### Is this a real session?

Yes. It is Henry Coutinho-Mason’s session “Thriving in the AI era” at IMEX Frankfurt 2025, as attendees saw it.

### Can I test Snapsight with my own speaker?

Yes. Snapsight runs it live with you, in the languages your audience needs, so you can judge it on your own audio and terms.

### Can I try it at my own event for free?

Yes. Live text and translation are free for every event, one session live at a time, so you can run Snapsight on a real session of your own.

### Can I see what Remix makes?

Yes. The experience page shows a report made with Remix from an IMEX session on AI and events, and a speaker card made with Speaker Remix in the event’s own branding.

### How do I book a demo for my event?

Use Book a demo and tell Snapsight about your programme and what you want attendees to leave with. The team walks you through live translation, takeaways and the content you could make with Remix.
