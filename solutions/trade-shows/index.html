Questions trade show organisers ask.

Our theatres are on the show floor. Does the noise matter?

Accuracy follows the audio. Take a direct feed from each stage’s sound desk, and we check every feed with you before doors open.

Do attendees need to download anything?

No. They scan the QR code or open the link in their phone’s browser. Or put the takeaways inside your show app, as IMEX did.

Can it run on every stage at once?

Yes. Tech Week Singapore put QR codes on all 17 stages, and ATxEnterprise ran all five. The $199 Engage event pass covers up to 10 rooms for up to 7 days; for bigger shows, talk to us about Enterprise.

Can we make reports for our sponsors?

Yes. Remix drafts post-event and sponsor reports from the sessions you pick, and the brief can carry your sponsors’ logos. AIME 2025’s report carried its partners on the cover.

Will we know which sessions people cared about?

At Tech Week Singapore 2024, organisers saw which sessions drew the most interest: 13,773 views from 1,540 active users.
