Questions
organisers ask.

What is free, exactly?

Live transcription and text translation for every event: unlimited sessions and hours, one session live at a time, and up to 10 languages per session from all 99. Attendees open a link or scan a QR code; there is no app. Voice translation, transcript downloads and Pro insights are paid.

How is listening different from reading along?

Reading along shows the speaker's words as live text in the attendee's language, free. Listening speaks them aloud in that language through the attendee's own earbuds, in the same 99 languages, at $10 per language, per session. Captions for that language are included.

Can it replace our interpreters?

Many events use Snapsight for every session that never had interpreters: breakouts, workshops, show-floor stages. Some keep interpreters for a flagship keynote and use Snapsight everywhere else. Either way, attendees choose their language on their own phone.

What do attendees need?

Their own phone and a browser. They scan the QR code on screen or open the session link, then pick a language. To listen, they use their own earbuds.

What does our AV team need to do?

Send the room audio from the mixer to a laptop running Snapsight, with a reliable internet connection. We check the feed, languages and attendee access with you before doors open.

Can we run several rooms at once?

Free runs one live session at a time. The $199 Engage event pass covers up to 10 rooms for up to 7 days. For larger programmes, talk to us about Enterprise.

Can attendees keep the transcript afterwards?

Free is for following live. Transcript downloads, summaries and live takeaways come with Pro sessions at $149 per session of up to 2 hours.
