## Frequently asked questions

### What does Snapsight do?

Snapsight builds AI for live events. It makes every session easier to follow while it happens, with live text, translation and takeaways on attendees’ phones, then makes what was said easy to find and use, with summaries, reports and speaker cards.

### Where is Snapsight based?

Snapsight is headquartered in Singapore. The team works from India, the United Kingdom, Europe and the United States.

### When did Snapsight launch?

Snapsight was first used at Business Events Industry Week in Chicago in April 2024, and launched officially at IMEX Frankfurt in May 2024. Remix followed in May 2026.

### Which products does Snapsight make?

Snapsight makes four products. Live translation lets attendees read free or listen for $10 per language per session; Takeaways and Idea Cloud give the key points during each talk; Remix drafts post-event reports, sponsor reports and decks; and Speaker Remix makes speakers’ own cards and posts.

### Which events use Snapsight?

Published stories include Economist Impact’s AI Innovation Asia, Informa Tech’s ATxEnterprise, Tech Week Singapore, ARC APAC Summit, The Decarbonised Mine and London Community Week, as well as IMEX, ICCA and The Meetings Show. Each story gives the event, what was run and what happened.

### Who does Snapsight partner with?

IAPCO names Snapsight a Service Provider Partner, the BestCities Global Alliance announced a two-year partnership in May 2025, and Freeman’s Key Takeaways for live events is powered by Snapsight. Agencies and AV companies resell it or run it under their own brand.

### How does Snapsight build its product?

Close to the event floor: the team spends time with organisers, speakers and attendees where Snapsight is used. Every insight stays tied to the session, speaker and moment it came from, and language, accessibility and format are part of the product from the start.

Yes. Open roles across sales, customer success and engineering are listed on the careers page, each with its location and working arrangement.
