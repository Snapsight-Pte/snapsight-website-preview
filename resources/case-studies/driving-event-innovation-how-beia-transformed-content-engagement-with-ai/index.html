BEIA Conference & AGM 2024Business Events Industry Aotearoa · New Plymouth

One volunteer
ran it for the whole conference.

After a 15-minute test at home, one volunteer ran Snapsight for 170 industry leaders.

Book a demo

The story

The challenge

Content capture relied on delegates’ own notes, and BEIA had no business case for recording every session. The AV team was wary of learning a new system in a short time.

What they ran

Snapsight, run by a single volunteer, with QR codes on the screens at the venue entrances and during breaks, information sheets on the tables and links in the conference app.

What happened

Delegates stayed present instead of taking notes, and BEIA kept the content to show what people missed and to promote future years.
