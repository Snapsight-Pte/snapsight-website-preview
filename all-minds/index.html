The All Minds initiative

Event knowledge that works
for every kind of mind.

A partnership that brings Snapsight to events built around neuroinclusion, at subsidised or no cost.

What you get
Snapsight for your events, subsidised or free depending on your situation, with the formats that serve your audience.

Who All Minds
is for.

Organisations where neuroinclusion is central to the mission. Free live text is already on every plan; All Minds goes further.

How Snapsight supports every attendee
Accessibility and neurodiversity organisations
Associations and non-profits focused on disability, accessibility or neurodivergent communities.
Healthcare organisations
Events for neurodivergent patients, caregivers, or the professionals who work with them.
Education and research
Conferences on inclusive education, universal design or cognitive accessibility.
Events that put cognitive access first
Any gathering designed with different minds in mind, where inclusive delivery is a priority.

What we want
to learn together.

There’s no playbook yet. With your consent, we’ll publish what we learn: a practical guide, templates and anonymised examples.

Apply to All Minds
  1. Which formats help most: summaries, structured notes, action lists or full transcripts?
  2. What reduces cognitive load without oversimplifying the content?
  3. How do language and format choices change engagement across audiences?
  4. What does good neuroinclusive knowledge delivery at events look like?
