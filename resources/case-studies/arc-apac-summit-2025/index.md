## The story

### The challenge

Medical talks are dense. Terms are complex, pronunciation varies by region and content shifts gears mid-session, and attendees expect value fast.

### What they ran

Real-time transcription and summaries across 20+ sessions for 300+ healthcare professionals, opened by QR code.

### What happened

By the end of the summit, 40% of attendees had used Snapsight. ARC invited Snapsight to its global summit in Tel Aviv in September 2025.
