Questions
security teams ask

Where can we get your SOC 2 report?

Request it from our trust centre at trust.snapsight.com, where our security documents are kept.

Do you use our sessions to train AI models?

No. Session audio, transcripts and everything generated from them are never used to train language models. That commitment is in our terms of use, privacy policy and subscription agreement.

Where is our data hosted?

In Frankfurt, Germany, by default. Regional data residency is available on request. Our privacy policy lists each sub-processor and where it processes data.

Who owns the transcripts and summaries?

You do. You keep ownership of the content you bring to Snapsight, and the transcripts, takeaways and summaries generated from it belong to you.

Can we have our content deleted?

Yes, at any time. Email dpo@snapsight.com to delete specific sessions or close your account, and we remove the content from our active systems. The privacy policy explains what is kept for legal reasons.

What happens if there is a security incident?

We notify you as soon as possible after we discover any security incident that affects your data.
