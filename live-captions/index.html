How do I add live captions to a conference?

Send each room’s audio from the mixer to a laptop running Snapsight and put the session’s QR code on screen. Attendees scan it and read live captions on their own phones as the speaker talks. Snapsight’s team checks the feed with you before doors open.

Can attendees get live captions on their own phones without an app?

Yes. They scan the session’s QR code or open its link in their phone’s browser, and Snapsight’s captions appear as the speaker talks. There is nothing to download and no devices to hand out.

Can we show live captions on the screen in the room?

Yes. Presentation Mode puts the live text full screen on a stage display, TV or monitor, in a high-contrast layout made for reading at a distance. Use it in the main room, in overflow rooms or on digital signage, while each attendee can still follow on their own phone.

Are live captions free?

Yes. Snapsight’s live captions and text translation are free for every event: unlimited sessions and hours, one session live at a time, and up to 10 languages per session. The $199 Engage pass covers up to 10 rooms at once, and transcript downloads come with Pro.

How accurate are AI live captions?

Snapsight’s English transcription is 97% accurate, measured on English sessions with a direct feed from the venue mixer, and speech becomes text in about 200 ms. Accuracy varies with audio quality, accents and language, so take the feed from the sound desk and add names and acronyms as custom vocabulary.

What is the difference between captions and subtitles?

Captions show the speaker’s words in the language they are speaking; subtitles translate them into another language. Snapsight gives attendees both live: captions in the spoken language, or translated captions in the language each attendee picks.

What is CART captioning, and when do we still need it?

CART (Communication Access Realtime Translation) is live captioning by a trained human captioner, booked per session. Book it when an attendee asks for a human captioner, or for a session where every word needs a person’s check. Snapsight’s AI captions can cover every other session, in every room with an audio feed.

Can live captions be translated into other languages?

Yes. Each attendee picks a language on their own phone and reads the captions translated as the speaker talks, with up to 10 languages per session. To hear the translation instead, voice is $10 per language per session, with captions included.

Will captions get speaker names and technical terms right?

Add speaker names, sponsor brands, drug names and acronyms as custom vocabulary before the session, and they come out right on every phone. Speaker detection shows who is talking on panels, and Snapsight keeps up when a speaker switches languages mid-sentence.

Can we download the captions as a transcript or subtitle file?

Yes, with Snapsight Pro. Transcript and subtitle downloads are included for purchased Pro sessions at $149 per session of up to 2 hours; Free and Engage are for following live.
