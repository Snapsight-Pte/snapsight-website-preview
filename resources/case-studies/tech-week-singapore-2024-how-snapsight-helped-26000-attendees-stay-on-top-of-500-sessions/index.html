Tech Week Singapore 2024Tech Week Singapore · Singapore

17 stages, 500+ sessions.
13,773 content views.

QR codes on all 17 stages gave 26,000+ attendees the takeaways from sessions they couldn’t reach.

Book a demo

The story

The challenge

500+ sessions in two days across 17 stages, for 26,000+ attendees and 550+ international speakers. Attendees missed valuable content, organisers struggled to track engagement across the stages, and any fix had to work in real time without adding work for speakers or attendees.

What they ran

QR codes on all 17 stages, one scan from each session’s takeaways. Snapsight Premium went further for attendees, surfacing the trends, opportunities, challenges and sentiment from the sessions they missed.

What happened

1,540 active users viewed session insights 13,773 times, about nine views each, spending 3m 27s on average. Organisers saw in real time which sessions sparked the most interest, and Tech Week Singapore plans to run Snapsight again.
