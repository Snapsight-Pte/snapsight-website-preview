What would I work on at Snapsight?

A product people use on stage, in the audience and after the event: live text, translation and takeaways on attendees’ phones during each session, and reports, posts and speaker cards afterwards. What the team builds shows up in the room, on screen while the audience is still listening.

Which roles are open?

The careers page lists every open role with its team, location and number of openings. Today they span sales, customer success and engineering, all remote and based in India.

Where can I work from?

Each job description lists its hiring location and whether the role is remote, hybrid or on-site. Working hours and any location requirements are specific to that role.

Will I need to travel to events?

Travel depends on the role. Check the job description for event attendance and travel requirements.

Which time zones does the team work in?

Open roles are remote in India. Customer-facing roles cover European and US business hours: customer success, for example, works 14:30–23:30 IST, Monday to Friday. Each job description gives its own schedule.

Can I apply for more than one role?

Yes. Submit an application from each relevant job page so the hiring team can consider your experience for that opening.
