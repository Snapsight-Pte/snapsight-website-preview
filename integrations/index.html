Can Snapsight work inside Cvent, Swapcard or our event app?

Yes. Snapsight works inside any event app that can embed a web page or open a link, including Swapcard, Cvent, Bizzabo, Whova, Grip, Brella, EventsAir, Hubilo, EventMobi and Stova. FIP ran it in its congress app, and IMEX, ICCA and UFI in theirs.

Is it a formal integration with those apps?

It doesn’t need one. Snapsight uses what event apps already support: embedding a web page or opening a link. Identiverse 2024 linked to its session reports from its own event app, and UFI’s tech team added takeaways to its conference app themselves, with no code.

Which parts of Snapsight can we embed in our app?

The session views: live text, takeaways, Idea Cloud and summary. Every session has its own link, the same one behind its QR code, and there is one link for the whole event.

Will attendees be sent out of the app?

Not if you embed it. If you use a link, your app team allows the Snapsight link, and most apps then open it in their own in-app browser.

Does Snapsight work for sessions on Zoom, Teams and Google Meet?

Yes. Snapsight takes the audio from online sessions on Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Google Meet, and remote attendees open the same session link as the room to follow live text in their language.

Which identity providers do you support for SSO?

Microsoft Entra ID (formerly Azure AD), Okta and other identity providers, for your team’s sign-in. Single sign-on is part of Snapsight Enterprise, alongside team administration.

Does Snapsight have an API or an MCP server?

Yes. Pull takeaways and summaries into your own systems over the Snapsight API, or connect your own AI assistant to your sessions over MCP and ask it about any session.

What if we don’t have an event app?

Attendees don’t need one. Without an event app, they scan the session’s QR code or open its link in their phone’s browser.
