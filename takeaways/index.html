Questions organisers ask

What does an attendee see?

A link or QR code for the session. It opens in their phone’s browser with the live text, takeaways, Idea Cloud and summary, all in the language they choose. There’s no app to download.

How many takeaways does a session get?

Usually around ten: a short headline and a sentence or two each, added as the speaker makes each point.

Where do the quotes come from?

From the stage audio. Every idea links back to the words that were said, with the speaker’s name on them. Speaker detection keeps names right on panels.

What do we need on site?

One audio feed from your sound desk, and the QR code on screen or in print. We test the feed and your languages with you before doors open.

Can the Idea Cloud go on stage?

Yes. Put it on the main screen, the LED walls or the foyer screens, in your event’s colours.

What happens after the session?

The takeaways and summary stay available, so people who missed it can catch up. They also feed Remix for your reports and your speakers’ posts.

Which languages?

99, for reading and listening. Each attendee picks their own.

What does it cost?

$149 per session of up to 2 hours, or less per session in packs of 10 or more. Live translation stays free.
