## Frequently asked questions

### Can an AV company or agency resell Snapsight?

Yes. Snapsight agrees packages and wholesale rates with you, so your team can add it to quotes and order forms like any other line. Attendees see Snapsight alongside your client’s event branding, and Snapsight’s team works with yours on event day.

### What’s the difference between reselling and white label?

Resellers put Snapsight on their own quotes at wholesale rates, and attendees see Snapsight alongside the event’s branding. With white label, attendees see your brand instead of ours.

### Can we white-label Snapsight completely?

Yes. Full white label removes “Powered by Snapsight” from what attendees see and runs on your own domain or subdomain. It’s part of Enterprise; your event branding is included on paid plans.

### What can carry our brand?

Your logo, colours and fonts go on the event page, the attendee view and the stage screen, including the Idea Cloud background. You can remove the QR code and the “Powered by Snapsight” line, and run it on your own domain or subdomain. Reports and speaker cards carry your client’s event brand, as IAPCO’s and London Community Week’s do.

### Can it sit inside our clients’ event apps?

Yes. Snapsight works inside any event app, embedded as an iframe or opened from a link in the app’s own browser. The app team whitelists one link.

### Can we connect to your API?

Yes. Pull takeaways and reports into your own systems over the Snapsight API, or connect your AI assistant to your sessions over MCP.

### Do you support our events?

Yes. Before doors open Snapsight checks the audio feed and languages with your crew, and its team supports event day with you.

### How are partner rates set?

Tell Snapsight your clients, your events and your yearly volume, and it proposes rates for your quotes. Enterprise includes wholesale options for AV and production partners.

### Is our clients’ content secure?

Snapsight is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified, and GDPR compliant. Your content is never used to train AI models, and your data, and the IP in it, belongs to you.
