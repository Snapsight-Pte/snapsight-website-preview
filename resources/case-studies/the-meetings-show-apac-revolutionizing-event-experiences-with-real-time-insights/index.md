## The story

### The challenge

The first Meetings Show APAC, at Marina Bay Sands, wanted to give attendees an immersive, engaging experience, and to get its main-stage content to the wider community who couldn’t travel to Singapore.

### What they ran

Snapsight captured all 23 main-stage sessions with no extra setup and produced key takeaways and Idea Clouds. Northstar built it into the programme: at the end of each session the host or MC highlighted the takeaways and invited attendees to scan the QR code.

### What happened

The takeaways sparked discussion in the room. After the show, Northstar and Snapsight turned the insights into a white paper, Asia Pacific’s role in recasting the global MICE sector, and sent it to the whole community by newsletter.
