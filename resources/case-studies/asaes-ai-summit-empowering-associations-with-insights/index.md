## The story

### The challenge

ASAE’s first AI Summit was a two-day virtual event for 500+ association executives and partners. On Day 1 it ran a survey on AI adoption in associations.

### What they ran

Real-time insights and summaries at the end of each session, and Snapsight analysis of the survey results.

### What happened

ASAE presented the findings in the Day 2 opening keynote, less than 24 hours later, then published the full report with sentiment by topic and job function.
