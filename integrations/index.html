Which event apps does Snapsight work with?

Any event app that can embed a web page or open a link, including Swapcard, Cvent, Bizzabo, Whova, Grip, Brella, EventsAir, Hubilo, EventMobi and Stova. IMEX, ICCA and UFI have run it inside their own apps.

Is it a formal integration with those apps?

It doesn’t need one. Snapsight uses what event apps already support: embedding a web page or opening a link. UFI’s tech team added takeaways to its conference app themselves, with no code.

Can we embed the Idea Cloud in our app?

Yes. The session views, live text, takeaways, Idea Cloud and summary, can be embedded directly in your app.

Will attendees be sent out of the app?

Not if you embed it. If you use a link, your app team allows the Snapsight link, and most apps then open it in their own in-app browser.

Do attendees need to download anything?

No. Without an event app, attendees scan the session’s QR code or open its link in their phone’s browser.

Does it work for sessions on Zoom, Teams and Google Meet?

Yes. Snapsight works with online sessions on Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Google Meet, and remote attendees open the same session link as the room.

Which identity providers do you support for SSO?

Microsoft Entra ID (formerly Azure AD), Okta and other identity providers, for your team’s sign-in. SSO is part of Enterprise.

Can we pull the content into our own systems?

Yes. Pull takeaways and summaries into your own systems over the API, or connect your own AI assistant to your sessions over MCP.
