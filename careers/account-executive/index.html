The opportunity

We are hiring three Account Executives to grow Snapsight with event organisers. You will understand what customers need, show how the product fits, and guide each opportunity through a pilot and a commercial decision.

This is a hands-on selling role. You should be comfortable learning the product, running your own demonstrations, following up with purpose and taking responsibility for the next step.

What you will own

  • Manage opportunities from discovery and demonstration through proposals, negotiation and close.
  • Build pipeline through relevant outreach, inbound enquiries, event conversations and referrals. Research the account before you make contact.
  • Understand the event programme, audience needs, content goals, buying process and the people involved in the decision.
  • Run focused product demonstrations and agree what a successful pilot would need to prove.
  • Handle questions about pricing, alternatives and timing with clear answers. Coordinate technical and procurement questions with the right teammates.
  • Keep HubSpot current with decision criteria, next actions and realistic forecasts.
  • Give customer success a useful handover: the promise made, the event requirements, the success measures and any unresolved questions.

What you will bring

  • Experience selling a B2B product or service, with examples of opportunities you have personally progressed and won.
  • Strong discovery skills: you listen, ask precise questions and connect product capabilities to a business need.
  • Confidence demonstrating software and learning enough of the product to answer practical questions.
  • Clear writing, reliable follow-through and the organisation to manage several opportunities at once.
  • Good commercial judgement and comfort discussing price, value and buying timelines.
  • Curiosity about AI, live events and how customers turn conversations into useful content. Event technology or SaaS experience is helpful.

What good looks like

  • Customers understand why Snapsight fits their event and what they are evaluating.
  • Your pipeline has credible next steps, clear decision-makers and dependable forecasts.
  • Pilots move toward a timely decision, and new customers arrive with the context the success team needs.

The hiring conversation

Be ready to talk through a sale you owned: how you found the need, handled a difficult moment and moved the opportunity forward. Our sales assessment uses a mock discovery and demo conversation to explore how you listen, respond to objections and agree a next step.

How to apply

Use the application form below. Share your CV, LinkedIn profile or portfolio link, and a short introduction if there is something you would like us to know. If someone introduced you to Snapsight, you can mention them in your introduction.
