Festival of CuriosityCuriosity of Linz23–24 May 2025 · Tabakfabrik Linz, Austria

Two people, 130+ activities. Months of content from one festival.

A two-person team captured a bilingual festival in German and English, Austrian dialect included.

Book a demo Start free

The story

The challenge

130 activities across AI, sustainability, food and the arts, in German and English including Austrian dialect, and no previous edition to learn from.

What they ran

Real-time bilingual transcription with speaker identification, and takeaways and Idea Clouds four to five minutes into each session, opened by QR code. Andreas started sessions; Selena edited in real time.

What happened

Summaries were ready by QR code right after sessions, bilingual reports were generated automatically, and the team has months of material for LinkedIn and other channels.
