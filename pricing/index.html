Questions organisers ask
before choosing a plan.

Choose the audience experience you need. Upgrade selected sessions to Pro.

What is unlimited in Free?

Live transcription and text translation have no monthly session or hour allowance. One session can be live at a time. Choose up to 10 active text languages from the full supported catalogue. Free includes live viewing with Snapsight branding, basic Q&A and manual polls. Voice translation, transcript downloads and Pro insights are paid capabilities.

Do I need Engage and Pro?

Pro includes everything in Engage for its own purchased session. The Engage event pass covers up to 10 rooms for up to 7 days, with all sessions and presenters. For a multi-room event with content on selected sessions, choose Engage for the event and Pro for those sessions. They are priced separately; Pro does not include an event-wide Engage pass.

What counts as an event or a session?

An Engage event lasts up to 7 days and includes up to 10 rooms, with all sessions and presenters. One Pro or Voice session is up to 2 hours. A longer session counts as two: it uses two Pro sessions from a pack, or costs $20 per voice language.

Can I download transcripts with Free or Engage?

Transcript and subtitle downloads are included only for purchased Pro sessions, including Pro sessions in Enterprise. Free provides live text viewing. Engage adds engagement analytics and exports of questions and poll results; it does not include the full spoken transcript.

How does individual Engage work?

Individual Engage is $19 per month for one presenter and one host account, with one live session at a time and unlimited attendees. It includes professional Q&A, polls, branding, engagement analytics and access to Speaker Remix; Remix credits are purchased separately. Cancel before the next renewal to stop monthly billing. It does not include Pro downloads or the automatic speaker content kit.

Do attendees or speakers have to pay?

Attendees join by link or QR code without buying a plan to view what you have enabled. Speakers can receive the automatic content kit from a purchased Pro session without an individual Engage subscription.

Is voice translation included?

Voice translation is an optional $10 per language per session on every plan, including Free. Each paid voice language includes its text captions. A session is up to 2 hours; a longer session counts as two. Three languages across five sessions of up to 2 hours cost $150. Voice does not unlock Pro transcript downloads or insights.

How are Remix credits used?

One credit creates one new standard text output. Downloads and manual edits use no credits. Each Pro session includes 5 credits and an automatic speaker content kit that uses no credits. Engage includes access to Speaker Remix, with credits purchased separately. An extra pack of 20 credits costs $19.

Do you offer special pricing?

Yes. Special pricing is available for nonprofits, associations, education and larger events. Enterprise includes volume rates and wholesale options for AV and production partners. Contact us to discuss your event or programme.
