Questions conference organisers ask.

Will it get our technical terms right?

Add drug names, acronyms and speaker names as custom vocabulary before the event. Accuracy follows the audio: 97% in English with a direct feed from the sound desk. ARC APAC Summit ran it across 20+ jargon-heavy healthcare sessions.

How soon is the summary ready?

Straight after each session, by QR code. Economist Impact had highlights out the same day, where edited recordings could take up to 48 hours.

Can attendees follow in their own language?

Yes, in 99 languages, each attendee on their own phone. Reading along is free; listening costs $10 per language, per session.

How much work is it for our team?

One audio feed and one QR code per room. At WiT Japan, one person ran AV, setup and integration, and was live within a week.

Can we get a report on just one track?

Yes. One session, a track, a day or the whole programme. Remix works only from the sessions you pick, and every quote carries its speaker and session.

Where does our session data go?

Snapsight is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified, and GDPR compliant. Session audio never trains models. Read about security
