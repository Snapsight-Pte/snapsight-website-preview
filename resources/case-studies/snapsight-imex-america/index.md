## The story

### The challenge

IMEX America drew 15,800 participants, including 5,758 buyers from 75 countries, and 3,500+ exhibiting companies, with 85,000 meetings on the floor. Attendees had little time for sessions, and the ones they wanted often clashed.

### What they ran

After its debut in the IMEX Frankfurt app, Snapsight returned inside the IMEX app with session takeaways, plus Snapsight Premium: deeper AI analysis, personalised intelligence reports for attendees and organisers, and monitoring tools for data-driven decisions.

### What happened

Attendees used the Snapsight notes to catch up on sessions they couldn’t get to, with key takeaways in the app for the majority of sessions.
