Does Snapsight work for a leadership meeting, not just a conference?

Yes. AICPA & CIMA used it at a two-day meeting of its Future of Finance Leadership Advisory Group, CFOs and senior finance executives from Fortune 500 and Global 1000 companies, and Snapsight drafted the meeting’s report.

Do we get a finished report?

You get a draft built from what was said, and your team finishes it. AICPA & CIMA’s team edited theirs and confirmed quote attributions with the members.

Can different audiences get their own report?

Yes. The Singapore Apex Business Summit 2024 had a summary after each day, then separate reports for the Ministry of Trade and Industry, the Ministry of Manpower, private-sector business leaders and the maritime and logistics sector.

Can Snapsight cover every breakout, not just the main stage?

Yes. Identiverse 2024 put a QR code at each session and had insights for all 250+ sessions over four days.

Do attendees need another app?

No. They scan the session’s QR code and read in their browser. Identiverse also announced it, and the session reports, in its own event app.

Where does our content go?

Snapsight is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified, and GDPR compliant. Your content never trains AI models, and your data, and the IP in it, belongs to you, transcripts and summaries included. Read about security

What does Snapsight cost for a corporate event?

Live text translation is free for every event. The $199 Engage event pass covers up to 10 rooms for up to 7 days, and takeaways, summaries and transcripts come with Pro at $149 per session. See pricing
