CityDNA International Conference 2025CityDNA · Budapest

An organising team of four.
65% of attendees used it.

Transcripts, summaries and translation across 15+ sessions, with a QR code printed on every badge.

Book a demo

The story

The challenge

Four people ran a conference for 150+ destination marketing professionals, and every session was packed with insights that can shift strategy. CityDNA needed each one captured accurately and in real time, without diverting resources it didn’t have.

What they ran

CityDNA first saw Snapsight at the AI-Opener for Destinations event hosted by GroupNAO, and brought it in as a sponsorship collaboration. It ran real-time transcripts, summaries and live multilingual translation across 15+ sessions with no one monitoring it, and a Snapsight QR code was printed on every attendee badge.

What happened

65% of attendees used Snapsight. They caught up on sessions they missed and rechecked key moments, speakers praised the accuracy of the summaries, and the team saved hours, maybe days, of post-event work. Next: more languages, Snapsight inside CityDNA’s event platform, and content before the event.
