What new sponsorship can we sell at a conference?

Sponsorship of the content itself. With Snapsight, a sponsor can present the takeaways, Idea Clouds and summaries from every session, and the post-event reports attendees come back to, with their logo on screens, in the app, on reports and on speaker cards. You sell it in your own tiers, at your price.

Where can a sponsor’s logo appear?

On the walk-in and QR slides, on the stage screen, in your event app, on report covers and pages, on a sponsor page inside the report, and on speaker cards and decks. The slides read “Educational Summaries presented by” your sponsor, and report pages read “Post-Event Insights presented by” them.

Who sells the sponsorship, and who sets the price?

You do. It sits in your own sponsorship programme, at the price you set, and Snapsight delivers the placements.

How should we price sponsored summaries and reports?

Against your existing tiers. Snapsight’s guidance is to price it above a logo placement of similar prominence, because the sponsor stays in view long after the event ends.

How can we package it?

As a premium add-on, as part of your Gold, Platinum or custom tiers, or as an exclusive for a single sponsor. One sponsor can take the whole event, or you can sell a different one for each room, stage or track.

Does the sponsor change the content?

No. Takeaways and reports come from what was said on stage, and the sponsor presents them. Their own message goes on their sponsor page, if you sell one.

Do you have wording for our prospectus?

Yes. Copy the Educational Summaries Sponsorship or the Post-Event Content & Reports Sponsorship from this page, then add your sponsor’s name and your standard benefits. Snapsight helps you fit them into your tiers on a call.
