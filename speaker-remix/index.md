## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get speakers to share our event on LinkedIn?

Give them something worth posting from their own talk. With Snapsight’s Speaker Remix, each speaker gets a private link, picks three takeaways from their session and gets a card in your event’s design plus a draft post they can edit. London Community Week made a card for all 49 speakers and shared them in 147 official LinkedIn posts across three Pages.

### What is a speaker card?

A branded image with one point from the speaker’s own session, their name, role and company, and your event’s name, dates and colours, with sponsor marks if you sell them. Speaker Remix makes one for each speaker from the session’s takeaways, alongside a draft post.

### Does anything publish without the speaker choosing to share?

No. The speaker selects the takeaways, reviews the card and draft post, and decides what to share. Opening a LinkedIn draft does not publish it.

### Does the content come from the actual session?

Yes. Speaker Remix starts from the takeaways Snapsight captured in the speaker’s own session, so the card uses the words they said. Speakers review the card and the post before anything is shared.

### Can the card carry our event identity and sponsors?

Yes. The London Community Week cards carry the event’s name, dates, colours and sponsor marks. Snapsight reviews your event’s assets and formats with you in a demo.

### How much work is it for our team?

One link per speaker. Your team captures the sessions, adds your logo, colours and sponsor marks, and shares each speaker’s private link; the speaker chooses the takeaways, reviews the card and edits the post. There is no design work for your team and no chasing speakers for posts.

### How do speakers get to their content?

Your team shares each speaker’s private link. They choose their takeaways, create the card and review the post on their own device, then download it or open a LinkedIn draft.

### How is Speaker Remix different from Remix?

Remix is for your team’s reports, posts and decks. Speaker Remix gives each speaker a focused way to turn their own session into a card and a post they want to share.
