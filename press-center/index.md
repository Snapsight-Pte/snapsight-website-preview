About Snapsight

Snapsight is AI software for live events. One audio feed from each room gives every attendee live text, translation, key takeaways, an Idea Cloud and a summary on their own phone, from a QR code or link with no app. Afterwards, Remix turns the same sessions into post-event reports, sponsor reports and decks, and Speaker Remix gives each speaker a branded card and post to share. Snapsight is headquartered in Singapore.

**Headquarters**

Singapore

**Products**

Live translation, Takeaways and Idea Cloud, Remix, Speaker Remix

**Launched**

May 2024, at IMEX Frankfurt
