Questions organisers ask
about Speaker Remix.

Does anything publish without the speaker choosing to share?

No. The speaker selects the takeaways, reviews the card and draft post, and decides what to share. Opening a LinkedIn draft does not publish it.

Does the content come from the actual session?

Yes. Speaker Remix starts from the session’s takeaways. Speakers review the card and the post before anything is shared.

Can the card carry our event identity and sponsors?

Yes. The London Community Week cards carry the event’s name, dates, colours and sponsor marks. We’ll review your event’s assets and formats with you in a demo.

How do speakers get to their content?

Your team shares each speaker’s private link. They choose their takeaways, create the card and review the post on their own device.

How is Speaker Remix different from Remix?

Remix is for your team’s reports, posts and decks. Speaker Remix gives each speaker a focused way to turn their own session into a card and a post they want to share.

Which plan includes it?

Engage includes the Speaker Remix workflow, with generation using Remix credits. See pricing for the current options.
