Questions
organisers ask.

What do attendees need?

Their own phone and a browser. They scan the QR code on screen or open the session link, then pick a language. To listen, they use their own earbuds. There’s no app.

Does it help attendees who are deaf or hard of hearing?

Yes. Live captions appear on their own phone as the speaker talks. Speaker detection shows who is talking on panels, and custom vocabulary keeps names and terms right.

Does it work for neurodivergent attendees?

Each attendee chooses how to follow: live text at their own pace, takeaways that build as the talk goes, the Idea Cloud, or the summary afterwards. They can step out and catch up. We don’t claim one format suits everyone; through All Minds we’re learning with organisers what helps most.

Can attendees catch up after a session?

Yes. Takeaways and summaries stay open, so people who stepped out or missed the session can catch up. Transcript downloads come with Pro.

Can remote attendees follow too?

Yes. They open the same session link and follow in their language, wherever they are.

What’s free?

Live captions and text translation, for every event: unlimited sessions, one session live at a time, up to 10 languages per session. Voice translation is $10 per language, per session, with captions included. Takeaways, the Idea Cloud and summaries come with Pro at $149 per session.

What is All Minds?

A partnership that brings Snapsight to events built around neuroinclusion, at subsidised or no cost. In return, you help us learn what actually helps, and we share it with the industry, with your consent.
