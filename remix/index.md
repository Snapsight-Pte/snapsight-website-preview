## Frequently asked questions

### How do I turn conference sessions into a post-event report?

Capture the sessions with Snapsight, then ask Remix for the report: say who it’s for and what it should cover, such as the themes across every stage with the quotes behind them. Remix drafts it from the sessions you pick, and you ask for changes until it’s right. IMEX Frankfurt 2025’s 47-page report covered everything said on stage.

### What can Remix make from event sessions?

Post-event reports, sponsor reports, LinkedIn carousels, one-pagers, decks, and versions in your audience’s language. If you can describe it, Snapsight’s Remix drafts it from your sessions, which makes it a quick way to repurpose conference content for marketing, members and sponsors.

### What does Remix work from?

Only the sessions you pick: their takeaways, quotes and transcripts, with speaker names and session details already attached. That can be one session, a track, a day or the whole programme.

### How do we check a Remix report is accurate?

Every quote in a Remix draft carries a speaker and a session, so your team can trace it back before anything is published. If something isn’t right, say so and Remix updates the draft.

### Can Remix follow our brand and editorial style?

Yes. Give Remix the tone, length, colours and sponsor logos in the brief, or ask for plain text to drop into your own templates.

### Can we make a sponsor report from our sessions?

Yes. Ask Remix for a sponsor report and put the sponsor’s logo in the brief; reports for The Meetings Show Asia Pacific 2024 and AIME 2025 carried their partners. Snapsight’s sponsorship page has two packages you can sell around it.

### Can we pull Remix content into our CMS or AI tools?

Yes. Pull takeaways and reports into your CMS over the Snapsight API, or connect your own AI assistant to your sessions over MCP.

### How much does Remix cost?

Each Snapsight Pro session ($149) includes 5 Remix credits, the 10- and 50-session packs include 50 and 250, and an extra 20 credits cost $19. One credit creates one new standard text output; downloads and manual edits use no credits.

### Is our content safe in Remix?

Snapsight is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified, and GDPR compliant. Your content is never used to train AI models, your data, and the IP in it, belongs to you, and Remix works only from the sessions you choose.
