The Meetings Show 2025Northstar Travel GroupExCeL London

Three stages at once. Every attendee got all 65 sessions.

Live text, key takeaways and Idea Clouds for every session on three main stages, readable in 99 languages.

Book a demo Start free

The story

The challenge

Three stages ran at the same time. The 1,500 planners attending could physically get to perhaps 8–10 of the 65 sessions, and language kept international attendees from following all of it.

What they ran

Live text, key takeaways and Idea Clouds for every session across the three main stages, opened in the browser in one click and readable in 99 languages. Setup took under five minutes, with no extra equipment.

What happened

Attendees went from 8–10 sessions to all 65. Northstar Travel Group is expanding its Snapsight integration for 2026, exploring workshops, exhibitor presentations and specialised tracks.
