Questions partners ask

What’s the difference between reselling and white label?

Resellers put Snapsight on their own quotes at wholesale rates, and attendees see Snapsight alongside the event’s branding. With white label, attendees see your brand instead of ours.

What can carry our brand?

Your logo, colours and fonts go on the event page, the attendee view and the stage screen, including the Idea Cloud background. You can remove the QR code and the “Powered by Snapsight” line, and run it on your own domain or subdomain. Reports and speaker cards carry your client’s event brand, as IAPCO’s and London Community Week’s do.

Can we remove the Snapsight name completely?

Yes. Full white label removes “Powered by Snapsight” from what attendees see and runs on your own domain. It’s part of Enterprise. Your event branding is included on paid plans.

Can Snapsight go on our order form?

Yes. We agree packages and rates with you, so your team can add Snapsight to quotes and order forms like any other line.

Can it sit inside our clients’ event apps?

Yes. Snapsight works inside any event app, embedded as an iframe or opened from a link in the app’s own browser. The app team whitelists one link.

Can we connect to your API?

Yes. Pull takeaways and reports into your own systems over the API, or connect your AI assistant to your sessions over MCP.

Do you support our events?

Yes. Before doors open we check the audio feed and languages with your crew, and our team supports event day with you.

How are partner rates set?

Tell us your clients, your events and your yearly volume, and we’ll propose rates for your quotes.

Is our clients’ content secure?

Snapsight is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified, and GDPR compliant. Session content is never used to train AI models.
