## Frequently asked questions

### How do we capture every seminar theatre at a trade show?

Give each stage one audio feed from its sound desk and put its QR code on the stage screen. Snapsight then gives attendees live text, translation and takeaways from every stage at once. Tech Week Singapore 2024 put QR codes on all 17 stages, and ATxEnterprise ran all five.

### Can attendees on the show floor catch sessions they missed?

Yes. Takeaways and summaries stay available, so attendees in meetings or on the floor can read the sessions they missed. At The Meetings Show 2025, every attendee got all 65 sessions, up from the 8–10 one person could attend.

### Our theatres are on the show floor. Does the noise matter?

Accuracy follows the audio, so take a direct feed from each stage’s sound desk rather than a room microphone. Snapsight checks every feed with you before doors open.

### Do attendees need to download anything?

No. They scan the QR code or open the link in their phone’s browser. Or put the takeaways inside your show app, as IMEX did.

### Can exhibitors and visitors follow sessions in their own language?

Yes. Each attendee picks a language on their own phone and reads the session as live text, free for every event. Listening to a translated voice through their own earbuds costs $10 per language, per session.

### Can we make reports for our sponsors?

Yes. Remix drafts post-event and sponsor reports from the sessions you pick, and the brief can carry your sponsors’ logos. AIME 2025’s report carried its partners on the cover.

### Will we know which sessions people cared about?

Yes. At Tech Week Singapore 2024, organisers saw which sessions drew the most interest: 13,773 views from 1,540 active users.

### What does Snapsight cost for a trade show?

Live text translation is free for every event. The $199 Engage event pass covers up to 10 rooms for up to 7 days, takeaways and reports come with Pro at $149 per session, and bigger shows talk to Snapsight about Enterprise.
